# Attribution and redistribution scope

No new redistribution license for the project's own research code or manuscript
has been selected here. Third-party files retain their original notices and terms.

- The manuscript includes the unmodified CVPR author style and bibliography style;
  upstream provenance is recorded in `paper/current/provenance.json`.
- SignSparK, SignSparK-BT and SignSparK Retrieval are external repositories with
  their own license files. They are cloned separately and are excluded from Git.
- SLRTP Evaluation is a separate external repository; consult its upstream terms.
- HumanML3D, its T2M evaluator, MDM and OpenAI CLIP are pinned external
  repositories with their own license notices. Code, downloaded checkpoints
  and the processed-motion mirror stay outside the Git export. Dataset access
  and redistribution follow the original HumanML3D/AMASS terms; the code license
  does not replace dataset terms.
- MLD, MotionLCM, T2M-GPT, MoMask, MotionRFT, TMR and MotionDiffuse are pinned external
  repositories. Their code and checkpoints are excluded from Git. In particular,
  MotionLCM's repository specifies non-commercial scientific research use;
  this project's export does not change those terms.
- MARDM is pinned separately under its upstream MIT code license. Its public
  essential-feature evaluator weights are downloaded from the author-linked
  Hugging Face release and are excluded from Git. No separate redistribution
  permission beyond the author model card (MIT) is asserted here.
- PHOENIX-2014T, How2Sign and CSL-Daily data and model weights are not included in
  this repository. Obtain them under their original access and license terms.
- The current teaser is a procedural rendering from native HumanML3D joint
  positions, with its source/equivalence checks recorded in the local artifacts.
  It is not a licensed SMPL mesh. Older photographs remain in historical local
  archives; this repository grants no separate license to dataset imagery.
- Research PDFs downloaded during the literature review are local archive assets,
  not part of the Git export. `docs/` records primary links and summaries.

The draft is anonymous and has no publication DOI or assigned submission ID.
